## Model Architecture

The project uses a U-Net-inspired architecture for semantic segmentation.

U-Net was introduced in the research paper:

**U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation**

Authors: Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer, and Thomas Brox (2015).

Although originally developed for biomedical image segmentation, U-Net is also widely used in remote-sensing segmentation research.
